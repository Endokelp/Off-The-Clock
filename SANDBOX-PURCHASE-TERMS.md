# Off the Clock sandbox purchase terms

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This page applies only to the Off the Clock sandbox checkout used to test the app during development and the RevenueCat Shipaton. Payments made through this checkout use Stripe test mode. No real money is charged, and a successful test payment does not create a real purchase or service contract.

Off the Clock helps a user organize their own work records and see applicable Washington employment rules. It does not give legal advice, make a claim, contact an employer, or act on anyone's behalf.

Use the sandbox checkout only for testing. Do not enter a real card number or other real payment information. The current sandbox test card is supplied by Stripe for this purpose.

The project is provided as a development demo without any promise of availability or suitability for a particular purpose. The full project source and contact route are available in the [Off the Clock repository](https://github.com/Endokelp/Off-The-Clock).
